# Legacy prototypes

Early stand-alone drafts of the budget, random-control, sensorimotor,
distillation, minimum-viable-montage and evaluation logic, written before the
runner in `src/` existed. Nothing imports them and they are not part of the
experiment path; they are kept for the history of the design decisions. The
maintained implementations are `src/channels.py`, `src/distillation.py`,
`src/metrics.py`, `src/kstar.py` and `src/analysis.py`.
